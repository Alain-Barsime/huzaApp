This is an app that helps in submitting local problems to suitable agencies for easy co-collaboration and cooperation between agencies and their people.

Features:

Any person can send a complaint without needing to login or signup, they report their problem and is able to be understood

Whats different with HuzaApp??


   There are some issues that require the departments where exactly?? In my app, for security of reporter, they just hit on button, I then capture current longitude and latitude, then there is map to agencies and departments where they see the exact location of the registration time, so if its an accident or death, they can know where, with security of reporter.


   Real time user friendly charts for agencies to track how well have they solved peoples issuess


   There is a feature where agency can write for more explanation on such complaint, i just keep track of case ID, all messages are based on case Id so receiver and sender can communicate through this without anything or security leak


   To enable people to track status of their complaints, at each case assigned, i give them a code for the case eg CASE-099r-erer2234


   AI-based analysis of described issue and it figures out which department needs to accept it, this would help local people who are not familiar with leadership hierarchy


   Everything is based on SQL databases where there is two-way communication btn reporters or complainers and agencies or responsible departments


   Good friendly fonts were used, i used also some timestamps but they are currently not accurate due to low time


   Dynamic backgounds through api to make stuffs cleaner and not boring



STATUS(S) THERE ARE THREE STATUS INDICATORS: not seen, in progress, resolved

        .not seen: this is when your issue hasnt been visited by any of the agency member

        .in progress:  when they read your file, then it means it is in progress of being resoved

        .resolved: If the agency confirms Mark if resolved button.


WHEN AGENCIES LOGIN TOO, THEY SEE A CHART SHOWING THEIR TOTAL RECEIVED COMPLAINTS AND HOW EACH STAGE OF STATUS OCCUPY IN OVERALL VIEW OF COMPLAINTS THEY RECEIVED


WHEN YOU GO TO TRACK YOUR COMPLAINT BY ID, YOU CAN ALSO WRITE FOR THEM AND YOU WITH ID WILL BE ONLY TO SEE RESPONSE IN THE SAME BOX
